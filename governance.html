Roles, criteria and responsibilities

Board of Patrons

Board members are prominent well-wishers who provide their consultancy on a voluntary basis. Their acts are conducted of their own accord, but shall be aligned with the organizational policy statement.

Duties

Strategic Guidance

  • Provide strategic direction and oversight to ensure that RVTI's goals and objectives align with its mission and vision.

Resource Mobilization

  • Assist in securing financial resources, grants, and donations to support RVTI's programs and initiatives.

Expertise and Advice

  • Offer expertise and advice on various aspects of RVTI's operations, including program development, partnerships, and community engagement.

Governance

  • Ensure good governance practices are followed, including transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct.

Mentorship

  • Provide mentorship and support to RVTI's leadership team and staff, helping them to develop their skills and capabilities.

Criteria

  • At least 3 members.
  • RVTI is a non-profit and a sister organization of Renaissance Language Campus (RLC).
  • A collection of individuals who bring unique knowledge and skills for effectively governing the organization.
  • Highly respected within and outside of RLC, well-experienced, knowledgeable and farsighted.
  • Committed and inspired to work on behalf of RVTI.
  • Value team spirit and mutual respect.
  • Have an altruistic sense.

Responsibilities

  • Give guidelines to make effective and efficient use of resources.
  • Give guidelines to achieve objectives and fulfill commitments to stakeholders.
  • Give guidelines to members to become more accountable to donors and other stakeholders.
  • Gain the respect and confidence of funding agencies, partners and beneficiaries.
  • Give guidelines to prepare for long-term financial sustainability.
  • Ensure projects are executed in a timely and consistent manner.
Director

The role of the Director is pivotal to the organization's success and overall functioning.

Duties

Leadership and Vision

  • Provide strategic leadership and direction to ensure that RVTI's mission and vision are effectively implemented.
  • Develop and execute long-term and short-term strategic plans to achieve the organization's goals.
  • Supervise operational management.
  • Oversee day-to-day operations, ensuring that programs and activities run smoothly and efficiently.
  • Manage staff, volunteers, and contractors, fostering a collaborative and productive work environment.

Financial Oversight

  • Develop and manage the organization's budget, ensuring financial stability, sustainability and value for money.
  • Oversee fundraising efforts, grant applications, and donor relations to secure necessary resources.

Program Development

  • Design, implement, and evaluate programs and initiatives that align with RVTI's objectives.
  • Ensure that programs meet the needs of the community and have a measurable impact.

Stakeholder Engagement

  • Build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders, including community members, partner organizations, donors, and government agencies.
  • Represent RVTI at public events, meetings, and conferences to promote the organization's work and build its reputation.

Governance and Compliance

  • Work closely with the Board of Patrons to ensure good governance practices are followed.
  • Ensure that the organization complies with all legal, regulatory, and ethical standards.

Communication

  • Serve as the primary spokesperson for RVTI, effectively communicating its mission, vision, and programs to diverse audiences.
  • Oversee the development and execution of communication strategies, including marketing, public relations, and social media.

Monitoring and Evaluation

  • Implement systems to monitor and evaluate the effectiveness and impact of RVTI's programs and initiatives.
  • Use data and feedback to continuously improve the organization's operations and expected outcomes.

Innovation and Adaptation

  • Foster a culture of innovation, encouraging staff and volunteers to explore new ideas and approaches.
  • Adapt to changing circumstances and emerging trends to ensure RVTI remains relevant and effective.

Responsibilities

  • Develop strategies for fundraising mechanisms with guidelines from the Board of Patrons.
  • Keep information on available sources of funding and donors up to date.
  • Develop appropriate proposals with guidelines from the Board of Patrons.
  • Set up meetings with donor agencies and organizations.
  • Design appropriate fundraising activities with guidelines from the Board of Patrons.
  • Maintain good relationships with donor agencies and organizations.
  • Arrange occasional meetings within RVTI on fundraising-related activities.
  • Develop project reports in line with donors' criteria.

Monitoring & Evaluation Officer

The M&E Officer is crucial for ensuring the effectiveness, efficiency, and impact of RVTI's programs and initiatives, and plays a vital role in ensuring they are effective, impactful, and continuously improving.

Duties

Monitoring

  • Develop and implement monitoring plans to systematically collect data on program activities and outputs.
  • Track progress towards the achievement of program objectives and goals.
  • Regularly update and maintain program databases and records.

Evaluation

  • Design and conduct evaluations to assess the outcomes and impacts of RVTI's programs and initiatives.
  • Develop evaluation frameworks and methodologies, including surveys, interviews, and focus groups.
  • Analyze evaluation data and prepare comprehensive evaluation reports.

Data Management

  • Collect, manage, and analyze data to provide insights into program performance.
  • Ensure data quality and integrity by implementing rigorous data validation processes.
  • Use data analysis tools and software to generate reports and visualizations.

Reporting

  • Prepare and submit regular monitoring and evaluation reports to the executive leadership and Board of Patrons.
  • Communicate key findings and recommendations to stakeholders to inform decision-making and program improvements.
  • Ensure compliance with donor reporting requirements and timelines.

Capacity Building

  • Provide training and support to program staff and partners on monitoring and evaluation best practices.
  • Build the capacity of staff to use M&E tools and techniques effectively.
  • Promote a culture of learning and continuous improvement within the organization.

Collaboration

  • Work closely with program managers and other team members to integrate M&E activities into program planning and implementation.
  • Foster strong relationships with external stakeholders, including donors, partners, and beneficiaries.
  • Collaborate with other organizations to share best practices and learn from their M&E experiences.

Learning and Adaptation

  • Identify lessons learned and best practices from program implementation and use them to inform future program design.
  • Facilitate learning sessions and workshops to disseminate key findings and promote knowledge sharing.
  • Adapt M&E approaches and methodologies in response to changing program needs and contexts.

Compliance and Standards

  • Ensure that all M&E activities adhere to ethical standards and best practices.
  • Maintain confidentiality and data security in accordance with organizational policies and regulations.

Criteria

Financial Management and Security

RVTI is a local non-profit organization established in 2009 and self-funded through members' contributions. We have established financial management standards and systems, and these must always be adhered to.

In 2023, RVTI underwent a reform allowing the organization to receive funding from external donors and organizations. This enables RVTI to implement specific interventions in project target areas and makes the organization eligible to submit proposals for external funding.

Members of RVTI — especially the Board of Patrons (BOP) and the Management Team (MT) — have a duty to maintain a collective bank account requiring the signatures of three guarantors for any cash withdrawal for project use (detailed in the RVTI Policy Statement). Funds and grants provided by donor agencies must be used only for the purposes for which they are intended.

Financial Management Blueprint

  1. Fund — Grants or self-generated income are received.
  2. Bank accounts — Funds are deposited into the organization's bank accounts (Thai-B or Myanmar-B).
  3. Budget & work plan — Spending is planned against an approved budget and work plan.
  4. Three signatures — Every withdrawal requires the signatures of three guarantors.
  5. Finance Team — Withdrawn cash goes to the Finance Team.
  6. Programme Team — Funds are released to the Programme Team according to the budget approved by the Director.
  7. Reporting — The Management Team reports to Monitoring & Evaluation.
  8. Board of Patrons — The BOP signs, receives reports and provides guidelines.
