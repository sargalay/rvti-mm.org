## Financial Management and Security

RVTI is a local non-profit organization established in 2009 and self-funded through members' contributions. We have established financial management standards and systems, and these must always be adhered to.

In 2023, RVTI underwent a reform allowing the organization to receive funding from external donors and organizations. This enables RVTI to implement specific interventions in project target areas and makes the organization eligible to submit proposals for external funding.

Members of RVTI — especially the Board of Patrons (BOP) and the Management Team (MT) — have a duty to maintain a collective bank account requiring the signatures of three guarantors for any cash withdrawal for project use (detailed in the RVTI Policy Statement). Funds and grants provided by donor agencies must be used only for the purposes for which they are intended.

## Financial Management Blueprint

1. **Fund** — Grants or self-generated income are received.
2. **Bank accounts** — Funds are deposited into the organization's bank accounts (Thai-B or Myanmar-B).
3. **Budget & work plan** — Spending is planned against an approved budget and work plan.
4. **Three signatures** — Every withdrawal requires the signatures of three guarantors.
5. **Finance Team** — Withdrawn cash goes to the Finance Team.
6. **Programme Team** — Funds are released to the Programme Team according to the budget approved by the Director.
7. **Reporting** — The Management Team reports to Monitoring & Evaluation.
8. **Board of Patrons** — The BOP signs, receives reports and provides guidelines.
