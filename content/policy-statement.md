## Introduction

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) is committed to fostering innovation, visionary thinking, and thriving communities. RVTI emerged as a rejuvenated and forward-thinking evolution of the former Renaissance Edu-Care, which was established in 2009 with the aim of supporting interventions in the education sector and environmental conservation, especially in Northern Rakhine State in Myanmar. The organization was self-funded but unregistered until 2023. While Renaissance Edu-Care laid a solid foundation by focusing on education and nurturing intellectual growth, RVTI takes this mission to new heights. The renaming to RVTI reflects a broader and more ambitious vision — one that not only emphasizes education but also champions innovation, creativity, and holistic development toward a peaceful, harmonious society.

RVTI aims to inspire individuals to think beyond traditional boundaries and embrace a renaissance of ideas and possibilities. By fostering an environment that encourages visionary thinking, RVTI empowers people to thrive in various aspects of their lives by promoting community rights to access education, resources and health care. The initiative focuses on cultivating the skills and mindsets that are essential for success in a rapidly changing world. This policy brief outlines the key policies governing our organizational practices to ensure alignment with our mission and values.

## Mission Statement

Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) aims to uplift communities in coastal states and regions in Myanmar through comprehensive programs that enhance educational opportunities, improve public health, and promote sustainable resource governance. We strive to foster self-reliance, resilience, and prosperity among individuals and families.

## Core Values

- **Innovation:** Encouraging creative solutions and forward-thinking approaches.
- **Visionary Leadership:** Cultivating leaders who inspire and drive positive change.
- **Community Engagement:** Building strong, collaborative partnerships within communities.
- **Sustainability:** Promoting practices that ensure long-term success and resilience.
- **All Inclusive:** Empowering communities to leverage their commonalities for comparative advantage, rather than focusing on their differences, towards a peaceful society.

## Organizational Structure

RVTI operates through a decentralized model to empower local leaders and ensure responsiveness to community needs. Key components of the structure include:

- **Executive Board:** Provides strategic direction and oversight.
- **Operation Team:** Offers expertise and guidance on specific initiatives.
- **Programme Team:** Designs and implements projects aligned with RVTI's mission to empower communities and drive sustainable resource governance.
- **Finance Team:** Ensures funding and budget are consistent and develops the annual budget in line with program goals and organizational priorities.
- **M&E Team:** Develops and implements an M&E framework to track RVTI's program performance and impact.
- **Local Chapters:** Implement programs and initiatives at the community level.
- **Logistics Team:** Oversees the procurement of goods and services, ensuring compliance with RVTI's procurement policy.

## Program Initiatives

- **Renaissance Visionary Leadership Programme:** Training programs for emerging leaders and capacity building for career and professional development.
- **Community Management:** Spaces for collaborative problem-solving, innovation and shared resource governance.
- **Sustainability Projects:** Initiatives focused on environmental and economic sustainability through diversified livelihood programmes.
- **Community Health and Hygiene Programme:** Empowers communities through awareness raising and provides the infrastructure needed to improve health outcomes and promote hygiene practices.

## Policy on Diversity and Inclusion

RVTI is committed to fostering an inclusive environment that values diversity. We ensure equal opportunities and access to resources for individuals from all backgrounds. This includes:

- **Inclusive Recruitment:** Active efforts to recruit a diverse workforce.
- **Cultural Competency Training:** Ongoing training to promote cultural awareness and sensitivity.
- **Equal Opportunity Programs:** Support for underrepresented groups in leadership and innovation roles.

## Policy on Community Engagement

RVTI places a strong emphasis on community engagement and collaboration. Our policies include:

- **Regular Community Consultations:** Engaging with community members to identify needs and co-create solutions.
- **Partnership Development:** Building strategic partnerships with local organizations, businesses, and government entities.
- **Transparency and Accountability:** Maintaining open communication and reporting on the impact of our initiatives.
- **Community Knows Better:** We are not here to teach communities what to do and what not to do; we are here to learn and facilitate.

## Sustainability Policy

We are dedicated to promoting sustainable practices across all our initiatives. Our sustainability policy includes:

- **Green Initiatives:** Implementing eco-friendly practices in our operations.
- **Sustainable Funding Models:** Ensuring long-term financial sustainability through diversified funding sources.
- **Impact Assessment:** Regularly assessing and reporting on the environmental, social, and economic impact of our programs.
- **Human and Ecological Well-being:** Human well-being and ecological well-being are equally important and must be well balanced.

## Evaluation and Improvement

RVTI is committed to continuous improvement. We regularly evaluate our policies and programs to ensure they meet the evolving needs of our communities. This includes:

- **Feedback Mechanisms:** Creating channels for stakeholders to provide feedback.
- **Performance Metrics:** Tracking key performance indicators to measure success.
- **Adaptive Management:** Adjusting strategies based on evaluation findings and emerging trends.

## Procurement Policy

RVTI's Procurement Policy is designed to ensure transparency, accountability, and efficiency in the acquisition of goods and services. It adheres to ethical standards, promotes fair competition, and prioritizes value for money while complying with donor requirements and organizational guidelines. Every purchase is carried out on the basis of three quotations. The policy emphasizes inclusivity by encouraging the participation of local vendors and suppliers, fostering community engagement and economic growth. Regular audits and reviews are conducted to maintain integrity and continuous improvement in procurement processes.

## Financial Policy

Financial security is our utmost priority. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy regarding financial misappropriation. All financial activities must be approved by both the patrons and the Director before execution. Any activity involving financial support must have documented evidence in accordance with our guidelines.

## Use of Office Property Policy

The Use of Office Property Policy ensures the responsible and efficient use of organizational resources. All office property — including equipment, furniture, vehicles and supplies — is to be used solely for official purposes in support of RVTI's mission, and a request letter must be submitted to use office property such as meeting rooms and vehicles. Our property policy treats everyone equally; no individual is prioritized on the basis of rank or position. Employees are expected to handle assets with care, report any damage, and avoid misuse or unauthorized removal. This policy promotes accountability, minimizes waste, and safeguards RVTI's resources for sustained operational effectiveness. Compliance with these guidelines reflects our commitment to integrity and stewardship, ensuring that every resource contributes to achieving our goals and serving the communities we support.

## Dissemination Policy

The Dissemination Policy ensures that information, knowledge, and resources are shared effectively and responsibly with stakeholders, partners, and the public. RVTI is committed to transparent, timely, and accurate dissemination of program outcomes, research findings, and best practices to maximize impact and foster collaboration. All materials must align with RVTI's mission, values, and branding guidelines, and be approved by designated authorities before distribution. This policy promotes inclusivity, ensuring that information reaches marginalized and underserved communities. By prioritizing ethical and strategic dissemination, RVTI strengthens its role as a trusted leader in driving sustainable development and empowering communities.

## Conflict of Interest Policy

RVTI is committed to maintaining the highest standards of integrity and transparency. The Conflict of Interest Policy requires all staff, board members, and partners to disclose any personal, financial, or professional interests, or affiliations with other organizations, that may influence or appear to influence their decisions or actions within the organization. Conflicts must be reported promptly and managed through recusal, mitigation, or other appropriate measures. This policy ensures that RVTI's operations remain impartial, ethical, and aligned with its mission. By upholding this standard, RVTI safeguards its credibility, fosters trust among stakeholders, and prioritizes the best interests of the communities it serves.

## Safeguarding Policy

RVTI is committed to ensuring the safety and well-being of all individuals — particularly children, young people, and vulnerable adults — who come into contact with our organization. This policy outlines our approach to safeguarding and the procedures we have in place to protect those at risk.

### Principles

1. **Equal Rights:** All children and vulnerable adults have equal rights to protection from harm.
2. **Responsibility:** Everyone associated with RVTI has a responsibility to support the protection of children and vulnerable adults.
3. **Duty of Care:** RVTI has a duty of care to those we work with, are in contact with, or who are affected by our work and operations.
4. **Best Interests:** All actions on child and vulnerable adult protection are taken in their best interests.

### Definitions

- **Children:** Individuals under the age of 18.
- **Vulnerable Adults:** Individuals over the age of 18 who have disabilities, either mental or physical, that put them in a position of vulnerability.
- **Representatives:** Employees, volunteers, and independent contractors (consultants) of RVTI.

### Prevention

- **Safe Recruitment:** All individuals working with children and vulnerable adults will undergo thorough background checks and safeguarding awareness training.
- **Code of Conduct:** All representatives must adhere to RVTI's code of conduct, which includes guidelines for appropriate behavior and interactions with children and vulnerable adults.
- **Training:** Regular safeguarding training will be provided to all representatives to ensure they are aware of their responsibilities and the procedures to follow.

### Reporting and Responding to Concerns

- **Reporting:** Any concerns about the safety or well-being of a child or vulnerable adult must be reported immediately to the designated safeguarding officer.
- **Response:** All reports will be taken seriously and investigated promptly. Appropriate action will be taken to protect the individual at risk and support those involved.

### Monitoring and Review

- **Regular Review:** This policy will be reviewed annually to ensure it remains effective and up to date.
- **Feedback:** Feedback from representatives and those we work with will be sought to improve our safeguarding practices.

### Responsibilities

- **Designated Safeguarding Officer:** Responsible for overseeing the implementation of this policy and handling safeguarding concerns.
- **Line Managers:** Ensure all representatives are aware of the safeguarding policy and procedures.
- **Team Members:** Adhere to the safeguarding policy and report any concerns.

## Conclusion

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative is dedicated to driving positive change through visionary leadership, innovation, and community engagement. By adhering to these policies, we aim to create a sustainable and thriving future for all.
