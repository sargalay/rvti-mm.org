About RVTI

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) — a local non-profit supporting education, public health and resource governance in Myanmar's coastal states and regions since 2009.

Our vision
To create a thriving, inclusive society where access to quality education, public health, and sustainable resources empowers every community to achieve their full potential towards a peaceful harmonious society.
Our mission
Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) aims to uplift communities in coastal states and regions in Myanmar through comprehensive programs that enhance educational opportunities, improve public health, and promote sustainable resource governance. We strive to foster self-reliance, resilience, and prosperity among individuals and families.

Background

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) emerged as a rejuvenated and forward-thinking evolution of the former Renaissance Edu-Care, which was established in 2009 with the aim of supporting interventions in the education sector and environmental conservation, especially in coastal states and regions in Myanmar. While Renaissance Edu-Care laid a solid foundation by focusing on education and nurturing intellectual growth, RVTI takes this mission to new heights. The renaming to RVTI reflects a broader and more ambitious vision — one that not only emphasizes education but also champions innovation, creativity, and holistic development toward a peaceful, harmonious society.

RVTI aims to inspire individuals to think beyond traditional boundaries and embrace a renaissance of ideas and possibilities. By fostering an environment that encourages visionary thinking, RVTI empowers people to thrive in various aspects of their lives by promoting community rights to access education and resources. The initiative focuses on cultivating the skills and mindsets that are essential for success in a rapidly changing world.

In essence, RVTI represents a commitment to continuous growth and adaptation, much like the Renaissance period itself. It symbolizes a dynamic and thriving community where individuals are encouraged to explore new horizons, collaborate on groundbreaking projects, and ultimately achieve their fullest potential by emphasizing shared commonalities rather than differences. With its renewed name and expanded mission, RVTI is poised to make a significant impact on the future of education and beyond.

Our History

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative, formerly Renaissance Edu-Care (REC), was established in Sittway, the capital of Rakhine State, in 2009 by senior members of Renaissance Language Campus who were inspired to engage in altruistic endeavors. As a locally established language center, Renaissance Language Campus is particularly motivated to work in the education sector and related fields, especially in creating an enabling environment that provides access to education for young learners who have been deprived of it due to various circumstances.

Since 2009, REC has been actively supporting local primary schools and monastic education centers with teaching and learning aids, funded entirely by its members' monthly contributions. Although REC's activities are primarily focused on rural areas near Sittway, it is also actively working in the countryside of Pauktaw and Minbya townships in Rakhine State.

REC initially agreed upon a clause that prohibited receiving funds and grants from external donors, as the organization was self-funded and its domain was educational support only. In 2011, REC reformed its policies and began collaborating with a mobile French team on environmental and sanitation concerns on an occasional basis. Since then, REC has adopted two working components — Education and Environment — towards fostering a peaceful and harmonious society through better resource governance by the community itself.

In 2023, the organization underwent a further reform, allowing it to receive funding from external donors and organizations. This enables RVTI to implement specific interventions in project target areas and makes the organization eligible to submit proposals for external funding.

Core values

Innovation
Encouraging creative solutions and forward-thinking approaches.
Visionary Leadership
Cultivating leaders who inspire and drive positive change.
Community Engagement
Building strong, collaborative partnerships within communities.
Sustainability
Promoting practices that ensure long-term success and resilience.
All Inclusive
Empowering communities to leverage their commonalities for comparative advantage, rather than focusing on their differences, towards a peaceful society.

Aims and objectives

Foster Innovation
Encourage and support innovative ideas and projects that align with the initiative's vision and mission.
Community Empowerment
Actively engage the community in the initiative's activities and decision-making processes to ensure that it meets the needs and aspirations of the people it serves.
Sustainable Development
Promote sustainable practices and projects that contribute to the long-term well-being of the community and the environment.
Education and Training
Provide educational and training opportunities to empower individuals with the skills and knowledge they need to thrive in a rapidly changing world.
Partnerships and Collaboration
Build strong partnerships with local, national, and international organizations to leverage resources and local expertise.
Cultural Enrichment
Support and promote cultural activities and programs that celebrate and preserve the community's heritage and diversity.
Economic Growth
Stimulate economic development by supporting local businesses and entrepreneurs, attracting new investments to the area, and improving access to markets.
Health and Well-being
Promote initiatives that enhance the physical, mental, and social well-being of community members.
Resource Governance
Empower communities to manage their own resources through existing customary practices and conflict resolution by strengthening formal and informal institutions in the communities.

How we work

Ground rules every member of RVTI follows.

Integrity and Transparency
All activities and operations will be conducted with the highest level of integrity and transparency.
Inclusivity and Equality
Promote inclusivity, equality, and non-discrimination in all programs and services.
Community Engagement & Empowerment
Actively involve community members in the planning, implementation, and evaluation of projects.
Sustainability
Focus on sustainable development practices that benefit current and future generations.
Collaboration
Partner with local, national, and international organizations to maximize impact and resources on a partnership platform.
Accountability
Maintain accountability to donors, beneficiaries, and stakeholders through regular reporting and audits.
Continuous Improvement
Commit to continuous learning and improvement through feedback and evaluation.
Cultural Sensitivity
Adhere to respect for diversity, inclusive language, cultural awareness, traditional customary practices, non-discrimination, adaptability, and reflection.
Value for Money
RVTI shall prioritize the optimal allocation and utilization of financial resources, ensuring they are used effectively and efficiently to maximize value and impact. Funds shall be managed with the highest level of transparency, accountability and integrity. All expenditures shall be subject to rigorous evaluation to ensure alignment with the initiative's goals and objectives.

Partner with us

We welcome partnerships with organizations and institutions that share our vision of enhancing education, healthcare, and resource governance. By working together, we can achieve greater impact and drive meaningful change in the communities we serve.
