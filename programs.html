Our programs

Education, healthcare, and resource governance and livelihood programs for underprivileged communities in Myanmar.

Project Scope

The Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative aims to bridge the educational, healthcare, and resource governance gaps in Myanmar, particularly in underprivileged areas. Our projects are designed to provide holistic interventions that encompass learning opportunities, healthcare support, and sustainable livelihood programs. By leveraging the experience and expertise of our team in resource governance projects, we are committed to driving positive change and fostering community development.

Education

  • Provide free access to learning English and other essential disciplines.
  • Offer learning assistance and resources to schools and institutions in rural areas, particularly in Rakhine State.
  • Develop and distribute educational materials and tools to enhance learning experiences.
  • Promote exchange learning among different schools and institutions across the country.

Healthcare

  • Implement healthcare programs aimed at improving the health and well-being of communities.
  • Conduct health education and awareness campaigns to promote healthy living practices.
  • Facilitate access to essential healthcare services and resources.
  • Raise awareness on hygiene and better public sanitation systems.

Resource Governance and Livelihood

  • Promote sustainable resource management practices in coastal states and regions.
  • Develop livelihood programs that support economic development and self-sufficiency.
  • Collaborate with local communities to ensure equitable distribution of resources and opportunities.

Programme initiatives

Renaissance Visionary Leadership Programme
Training programs for emerging leaders and capacity building for career and professional development.
Community Management
Spaces for collaborative problem-solving, innovation and shared resource governance.
Sustainability Projects
Initiatives focused on environmental and economic sustainability through diversified livelihood programmes.
Community Health and Hygiene Programme
Empowers communities through awareness raising and provides the infrastructure needed to improve health outcomes and promote hygiene practices.

Partner with us

We welcome partnerships with organizations and institutions that share our vision of enhancing education, healthcare, and resource governance. By working together, we can achieve greater impact and drive meaningful change in the communities we serve.
