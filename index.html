A local initiative since 2009

Members of Renaissance Language Campus started Renaissance Edu-Care (REC) in Sittway in 2009, paying for school supplies out of their own pockets. Today RVTI works on education, public health and community-led resource governance across Myanmar's coast.

Read our story

Our vision
To create a thriving, inclusive society where access to quality education, public health, and sustainable resources empowers every community to achieve their full potential towards a peaceful harmonious society.
Our mission
Renaissance Visionary Thriving Initiative (RVTI) aims to uplift communities in coastal states and regions in Myanmar through comprehensive programs that enhance educational opportunities, improve public health, and promote sustainable resource governance. We strive to foster self-reliance, resilience, and prosperity among individuals and families.

What we do

The three figures in our logo stand for the three areas we work in.

Education

  • Provide free access to learning English and other essential disciplines.
  • Offer learning assistance and resources to schools and institutions in rural areas, particularly in Rakhine State.
  • Develop and distribute educational materials and tools to enhance learning experiences.
More on education

Healthcare

  • Implement healthcare programs aimed at improving the health and well-being of communities.
  • Conduct health education and awareness campaigns to promote healthy living practices.
  • Facilitate access to essential healthcare services and resources.
More on healthcare

Resource Governance and Livelihood

  • Promote sustainable resource management practices in coastal states and regions.
  • Develop livelihood programs that support economic development and self-sufficiency.
  • Collaborate with local communities to ensure equitable distribution of resources and opportunities.
More on resource governance and livelihood

Where we work

  • Rakhine State

    Our home state and main focus. Founded in Sittway; offices in Sittway and Ponnagyun.

  • Ayeyarwady Region

    Myanmar's rice-growing delta, where fishers and smallholders face conflicts over resource access.

  • Tanintharyi Region

    The southernmost coast and the Mergui Archipelago, rich in fisheries and forests.
